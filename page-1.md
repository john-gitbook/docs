# Page 1

Lankum are a contemporary Irish [folk music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Folk\_music) group from [Dublin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dublin), consisting of multi-instrumentalists Ian Lynch, Daragh Lynch, Cormac MacDiarmada and Radie Peat.

Originally a duo consisting of the Lynch brothers, known as Lynched, the pair released their debut album, _Where Did We Go Wrong?!_ in 2003. Returning to Ireland, after a spell of international touring, the brothers deepened their interest in Dublin's [Irish traditional music sessions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irish\_traditional\_music\_session) which introduced them to vocalist and multi-instrumentalist Radie Peat and violinist Cormac MacDiarmada.

Recording as the core four-piece band, they released the albums _Cold Old Fire_ in 2014 and [_Between the Earth and Sky_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Between\_the\_Earth\_and\_Sky\_\(Lankum\_album\)) in 2017. In 2018, they were named Best Folk Group at the [RTÉ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RT%C3%89) Folk Music Awards, while Radie Peat was named Best Folk Singer.[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lankum#cite\_note-rte-1) The band were nominated for the RTÉ [Choice Music Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Choice\_Music\_Prize) Irish Album of the Year in 2017 for their album [_Between the Earth and Sky_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Between\_the\_Earth\_and\_Sky\_\(Lankum\_album\)), and won the prize in 2019 and 2024 for their albums [_The Livelong Day_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The\_Livelong\_Day) and [_False Lankum_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/False\_Lankum).[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lankum#cite\_note-choiceprize-2)

The band's fourth studio album, [_False Lankum_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/False\_Lankum) (2023), was released to widespread critical acclaim and increased the band's exposure significantly. It was nominated for the [Mercury Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercury\_Prize) and placed highly on several end-of-year lists. In 2024 the band released a live album [_Live in Dublin_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Live\_in\_Dublin\_\(Lankum\_album\)), recorded across three sold out nights at Dublin's Vicar Street.
